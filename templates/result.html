Early Blight

A common fungal disease of tomato plants

Common symptoms

  • Brown spots with circular, target-like rings on older leaves.
  • Yellowing around affected areas.
  • In severe cases, leaves may dry and fall off.

Prevention and care

  • Remove heavily affected leaves and dispose of them properly.
  • Water near the soil instead of wetting the leaves.
  • Maintain good spacing and airflow between plants.
  • Rotate crops and avoid planting tomatoes in the same soil repeatedly.

Late Blight

A disease that can spread rapidly in cool, wet conditions

Common symptoms

  • Water-soaked, irregular dark patches on leaves.
  • White growth may appear around affected areas in humid conditions.
  • Dark lesions can develop on stems and fruit.

Prevention and care

  • Inspect plants regularly, especially after wet weather.
  • Remove and dispose of infected plant material safely.
  • Avoid overhead watering and improve airflow.
  • Seek local agricultural advice if the disease is spreading quickly.
